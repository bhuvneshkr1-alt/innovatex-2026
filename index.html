About

InnovateX is the flagship event of eCell NSUT, the Entrepreneurship Cell of Netaji Subhas University of Technology. It brings together students with fresh ideas and bold ambitions, giving them a platform to compete, learn and build. Through case studies, pitches and hands-on challenges, InnovateX aims to nurture an innovation mindset and strengthen the startup ecosystem. Invent. Incubate. Inspire.
